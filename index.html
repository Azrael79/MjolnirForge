The complete training system

Built for the events you will actually face

Mjölnir Forge is designed around the demands of strongman competition rather than adapting a generic lifting template after the fact.

01

Competition Planning

Enter your show date, events, implements, rules, target weights, distances, equipment, experience, and available training days.

02

Event-Aware Programming

Prepare for log, axle, yoke, farmers, stones, sandbags, deadlifts, medleys, carries, and other competition demands.

03

Purposeful Guidance

See the purpose of the day, target RPE, workload intent, suggested rest, exercise instructions, and coaching cues.

04

Training Log & Progress

Record sets, repetitions, load, distance, time, effort, and notes while building a history of completed work.

05

Phased Preparation

Move through base, build, peak, and taper phases that bring training toward the demands of competition day.

06

Local-First by Design

Your core athlete profile, programs, and workout logs stay on your device rather than requiring an account or constant backend connection.

Does Mjölnir Forge require an account?

No separate Mjölnir Forge account is required for the current local-first version. Core profile, program, and workout information is stored on your device.

How do I restore a subscription?

Confirm that you are signed into the Apple ID used for the purchase, then use Restore Purchases or refresh the App Store entitlement from the Subscription area in the app.

What happens if I delete the app?

Locally stored app data may be removed when the app is deleted. Data may only return if restored through an applicable device backup or supported backup feature.

How do I manage or cancel billing?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Mjölnir Forge, and manage the subscription through Apple.

Can the app replace a coach or clinician?

No. Use your own judgment and qualified professional guidance, especially for injuries, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, or symptoms that may require medical care.
