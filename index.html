The complete training system

More than a generic workout tracker

Mjölnir Forge is designed around the demands of strongman and strongwoman competition—not retrofitted from bodybuilding templates.

01

Competition-Specific Programs

Build a structured plan from your competition date, events, implements, target weights, equipment, experience, and schedule.

02

Event Preparation

Prepare for log, axle, yoke, farmers, stones, sandbags, deadlifts, medleys, carries, and other event demands.

03

Purposeful Training Guidance

See the purpose of the day, target RPE, workload intent, suggested rest, exercise instructions, and coaching cues.

04

Training Log & Progress

Record sets, reps, load, distance, time, effort, and notes while building a history of the work you have completed.

05

Phased Preparation

Move through base, build, peak, and taper phases designed to bring training toward the demands of competition day.

06

Local-First by Design

Your core profile, programs, and workout logs remain on your device rather than requiring an account or constant backend connection.

Inclusive training support

Mjölnir Forge is designed for strongman and strongwoman athletes across experience levels. Pregnancy and postpartum modes provide conservative educational guidance and symptom-aware scaling for users who have appropriate clinical clearance.

Training and health notice

Mjölnir Forge is a planning and educational tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, emergency support, or a substitute for a qualified clinician or coach.

Help when you need it

Start with the quick answers below or contact Forge & Frequency LLC directly.

Contact support

Include what you were doing, what happened, your iPhone model, iOS version, and any message shown in the app.

support@mjolnirforge.com Typical response target: within two business days.
Does Mjölnir Forge require an account?

No separate Mjölnir Forge account is required for the current local-first version. Core profile, program, and workout information is stored on your device.

How do I restore a subscription?

Confirm that you are signed into the Apple ID used for the purchase, then use Restore Purchases or refresh the App Store entitlement from the Subscription area in the app.

What happens if I delete the app?

Locally stored app data may be removed when the app is deleted. Data may only return if restored through an applicable device backup or future backup feature.

How do I manage or cancel billing?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Mjölnir Forge, and manage the subscription through Apple.

Can the app replace a coach or clinician?

No. Use your own judgment and qualified professional guidance, especially for injuries, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, or symptoms that may require medical care.
