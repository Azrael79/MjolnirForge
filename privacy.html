Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mjölnir Forge handles information in the iOS app, on this website, and when you contact support.

Effective: August 3, 2026Contact: support@mjolnirforge.com
SectionsSummaryLocal app dataCollectionPurchasesSupportWebsiteSharingRetentionYour choicesChildrenChangesContact

1. Summary

Mjölnir Forge is developed and provided by Forge & Frequency LLC (“Forge & Frequency,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). The current app is designed as a local-first training tool. Core athlete profiles, programs, competition details, and workout logs are stored on your device rather than in a Mjölnir Forge account or backend database.

We do not sell personal information. The current app does not use third-party advertising SDKs and is not designed to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

2. Information Stored Locally in the App

Depending on the features you use, Mjölnir Forge may store the following on your device:

  • Athlete profile information, including name, age, gender, body weight, preferred units, experience, training schedule, equipment, limitations, and training maxes.
  • Competition and program information, including dates, events, implements, target loads, distances, rules, phases, exercises, sets, repetitions, rest, substitutions, and coaching notes.
  • Workout information, including completed sessions, load, repetitions, RPE, time, distance, notes, and timestamps.
  • Pregnancy and postpartum settings and check-ins you choose to use, including stage, clearance status, symptoms, recovery considerations, and related notes.
  • App settings, progress, achievements, and entitlement status.

This information remains under your control on the device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or include it in a support request.

3. Information Collected by Forge & Frequency

The current release does not require a Mjölnir Forge account and does not send your athlete profile, generated programs, workout logs, pregnancy or postpartum entries, contacts, photos, precise location, or HealthKit data to a Forge & Frequency server.

App behavior may change as features are added. Before introducing analytics, cloud synchronization, advertising, or another material data practice, we will update this policy and the app’s privacy disclosures as appropriate.

4. App Store Purchases and Subscriptions

Apple processes App Store downloads, purchases, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. Mjölnir Forge uses Apple’s StoreKit services to determine whether paid features should be available. Apple may provide transaction and entitlement information such as product identifier, transaction identifier, purchase date, expiration date, revocation status, and environment.

Forge & Frequency does not receive your complete payment-card number or Apple ID password. Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s own terms and privacy practices.

5. Support Communications

When you email support, we receive the email address used, your message, and any screenshots, diagnostic details, training information, health-related information, or other content you choose to send. We use this information to investigate, respond to, document, and resolve the request.

Do not send emergency information or unnecessary sensitive health information. Mjölnir Forge support is not a medical or emergency service.

6. Website Information

This public website is primarily informational. The current static site does not provide user accounts or directly request payment information. The hosting provider may automatically process ordinary technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser type, requested page, referring page, and access time. Such processing is governed in part by the hosting provider’s practices.

If analytics, contact forms, cookies beyond essential hosting functions, or marketing tools are later added, this policy will be updated before or when those tools are deployed.

7. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary:

  • With Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, subscriptions, refunds, and platform services.
  • With email, hosting, or support providers that process information on our behalf.
  • To comply with law, legal process, or lawful government requests.
  • To protect users, Forge & Frequency, or others from fraud, abuse, security threats, or harm.
  • In connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of business assets, subject to appropriate notice and protections.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it through an available app control, reset the app, or delete the app. Deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored information unless it is included in an applicable device backup or supported backup feature.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain business records, resolve disputes, protect security, and satisfy legal obligations. You may request deletion of support information by contacting us, subject to legal and operational exceptions.

9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You control what information you enter in the app and what you include in support messages. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a portable copy of personal information we hold. Because most app data is stored locally, many choices can be exercised directly on your device.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

10. Children

Mjölnir Forge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users who are minors under applicable local law should use the app only with appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information and services involved. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International Use

Forge & Frequency is based in the United States. Information submitted to support or processed by service providers may be handled in the United States or other countries where those providers operate, subject to applicable law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Mjölnir Forge changes. The effective date above will identify the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through the website, app, or another appropriate method.
